# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard (2022)

An interactive business intelligence dashboard built to analyze e-commerce performance across products, cities, categories, and time. The dashboard consolidates sales, profit, and order data into a single visual report to support data-driven decision-making.

 
## 📌 Overview

This project presents a complete **Ecommerce Data Dashboard** for the year 2022, designed to track and visualize key business metrics such as sales, profit, order volume, and customer activity. The dashboard enables stakeholders to quickly identify top-performing products, high-revenue cities, and seasonal sales trends, while also allowing filtering by month, order status, payment method, and customer.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Consolidate raw e-commerce transaction data into a single, easy-to-read visual report
- Identify top and bottom performing products by profit
- Track monthly sales trends to detect seasonality
- Compare profit and sales performance across cities
- Break down sales contribution by product category
- Provide interactive filters for deeper, self-service analysis

## 📈 Dashboard Components

- **Product Wise Profit** — Treemap visualization ranking products (Table, Shirt, Lamp, Laptop, Mouse, Jeans, Bottle, Desk, Phone, Chair, Jacket, Mixer, Keyboard) by profit contribution.
- **Month Wise Sales** — Bar chart showing sales trends from January to December, highlighting peak and low sales months.
- **Category Wise Sales** — Donut chart breaking down sales share across Electronics, Furniture, Clothing, and Home categories.
- **City Wise Profit** — Bar chart comparing total profit across Lahore, Rawalpindi, Karachi, Islamabad, and Faisalabad.
- **City Wise Total Profit & Sales** — Combo chart comparing profit vs. sales performance by city.
- **Interactive Filters** — Slicers for Month, Order Status, Payment Method (Bank, Card, Cash, PayPal), and Customer Name.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Furniture items such as Tables, Jeans, and Chairs generated the highest profit margins.
- Sales peaked between January and May, with a noticeable decline through the second half of the year.
- Lahore recorded the highest profit and sales among all cities, followed closely by Rawalpindi and Karachi.
- Electronics and Furniture together account for nearly half of total category-wise sales.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the `.pbix` file in Power BI Desktop.
3. Use the slicers (Month, Status, Payment Method, Customer Name) to filter and explore the data interactively.
4. Hover over visuals to view detailed tooltips for each data point.
